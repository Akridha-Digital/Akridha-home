PerHead · Privacy Policy

Your money data, handled with care

Last updated: 27 November 2026 · Applies to the PerHead mobile app

The short version

  • You create an account with your email and a password. Passwords are stored only in hashed form.
  • We store the expenses, groups and splits you create so the app can sync and share them with the people you split with.
  • When you add an expense by voice or scan a receipt, the audio or image is sent to Google's Gemini AI to read it. We keep the resulting expense details, not the recording or photo.
  • We use Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics to improve the app, and Google AdMob to show ads.
  • We never sell your personal data.
  • You can delete your account and data at any time from inside the app. See how.

01Who we are

PerHead is an expense tracking and bill-splitting app published by AKRIDHA DIGITAL, a registered software business in Kanpur, Uttar Pradesh, India ("we", "us", "our"). This policy explains what information PerHead collects, why, who it is shared with, and the choices you have. By using PerHead you acknowledge this policy. If you do not agree with it, please do not use the app.

02Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Account details: your email address, the name you choose to display, and your password. Passwords are stored only as a secure one-way hash; we cannot read them.
  • Expense and group data: expenses you add (amount, description, category, date, who paid and how it is split), groups you create, settle-up records, and notes.
  • People you split with: the names and, if you enter them, email addresses of friends or group members you add, so they can be included in splits and invited to see shared expenses.
  • Voice input and receipt photos: when you choose to add an expense by speaking or by scanning a receipt (see section 3).
  • Support messages: anything you send us when you contact support.

Information collected automatically

  • App usage and diagnostics: screens viewed, features used, app version, device model, operating system and approximate region, collected through Firebase Analytics.
  • Crash reports: crash logs, stack traces and device state at the time of a crash, collected through Firebase Crashlytics.
  • Notification token: a device token from Firebase Cloud Messaging so we can send you reminders and split updates.
  • Advertising data: your device advertising ID and ad interactions, collected by Google AdMob (see section 7).

We do not collect your precise location, your contacts list, or any payment card or bank details. PerHead records expenses; it does not move money.

03Voice and receipt processing (AI)

PerHead can read expenses from your voice or from a photo of a receipt. These features only run when you start them.

  • Voice: when you tap the microphone, the audio you record is sent securely to Google's Gemini API, which converts it into expense details (for example amount, description and who was involved).
  • Receipts: when you scan a receipt, the photo is sent to the Gemini API to read the merchant, items and total.
  • Search and categories: the text of your expenses may be sent to the Gemini API to suggest categories or answer natural-language searches inside the app.
We store the expense details produced from your voice or receipt. We do not store your audio recordings or receipt photos on our servers after processing. Google processes this data as our service provider under the Gemini API Additional Terms of Service.

Please avoid speaking or photographing information you do not want processed, such as card numbers or other people's private details.

04How we use information

  • to create and secure your account and keep you signed in;
  • to record, sync and back up your expenses, groups and balances across your devices;
  • to show shared expenses and balances to the members of a group you share them with;
  • to turn voice and receipt input into expense entries, and to suggest categories;
  • to send notifications you have allowed, such as reminders and split updates;
  • to fix bugs, prevent abuse and improve the app, using analytics and crash data;
  • to show ads that keep PerHead free; and
  • to reply to support requests and meet legal obligations.

We do not use your expense data to build advertising profiles, and we do not sell it.

06Device permissions

  • Microphone: only while you use voice entry.
  • Camera: only while you scan a receipt.
  • Notifications: to send reminders and split updates.
  • Internet: to sync your data and load ads.

You can refuse or withdraw any permission in your device settings. The related feature will stop working, but the rest of the app will still work.

07Advertising

PerHead is free and shows ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may use your device advertising ID and similar data to show, limit and measure ads and to prevent fraud. Where required by law (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), we ask for your consent before personalised ads are shown.

You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads on Android at Settings → Privacy → Ads (or Settings → Google → Ads). You will still see ads, but they will be less tailored.

08Data retention

  • Account and expense data: kept while your account is active, and deleted within 30 days after you delete your account.
  • Voice recordings and receipt photos: not stored by us after processing.
  • Analytics and crash data: kept by Firebase under its retention settings, in a form not used to identify you directly.
  • Backups: deleted data may remain in encrypted backups for up to a further 30 days before it is overwritten.
  • Legal records: we may keep limited information longer where the law requires it.

09Deleting your account and data

Delete from inside the app

  1. Open PerHead and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

No longer have the app?

Email us from the address linked to your PerHead account and we will delete it for you.

Request deletion by email

What is deleted: your account, profile, email address, password hash, and the expenses, groups and settle-ups you created. Data is removed from our live systems straight away and from backups within 30 days.

What may remain: expenses you added to a shared group may still show for the other members, with your name replaced by "Deleted user", so their balances stay correct. Anonymous analytics and crash data, and records we must keep by law, may also be retained.

10Security

Data is sent over encrypted HTTPS connections. Passwords are hashed, access to our servers and database is restricted, and data is stored with hosting providers that use industry-standard security. No system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. If a breach affects your personal data, we will notify you and the relevant authority as required by law.

11Your rights

Depending on where you live, including under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and, where applicable, the GDPR, you may have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you;
  • correct inaccurate data (you can edit most of it in the app);
  • delete your data (see section 9);
  • withdraw consent, such as for personalised ads or permissions;
  • object to or restrict certain processing, or receive a copy of your data; and
  • complain to a data protection authority.

To use these rights, email akridhadigital@gmail.com from your account email. We will respond within the time required by law.

12Children

PerHead is not intended for children under 18, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

13International transfers

We are based in India. Our service providers, including Google, Render and Microsoft, may store and process data in other countries, including the United States. Where required, these transfers are protected by appropriate safeguards offered by those providers.

14Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when PerHead changes. The new version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date, and significant changes will also be shown in the app. Continuing to use PerHead after an update means you accept the revised policy.
